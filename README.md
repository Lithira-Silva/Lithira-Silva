## 🚀 About Me

<img align="right" alt="Coding" width="400" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/qgQUggAC3Pfv687qPC/giphy.gif">

Hey there—I'm **Lithira Silva**, a passionate digital creator, UI/UX enthusiast, and relentless innovator at the intersection of design and technology. My work thrives on sleek interfaces, bold aesthetics, and immersive user experiences, inspired by cyberpunk culture and the ever-evolving world of modern design.

With a solid foundation in front-end development and a keen eye for visual storytelling, I transform complex ideas into intuitive, beautiful digital products. Whether it's crafting pixel-perfect websites, designing interactive prototypes, or pushing the boundaries of creative code, I believe in design that resonates—visually, emotionally, and functionally.

### ⚡ What sets me apart?

- **🎯 Cutting-Edge Design:** I blend minimalist principles with vibrant, futuristic elements to create interfaces that captivate and engage
- **👥 Human-Centered Approach:** Every project starts with empathy, ensuring solutions that are as user-friendly as they are visually striking  
- **🚀 Continuous Innovation:** I stay ahead of trends, mastering new tools and techniques to deliver fresh, impactful results
